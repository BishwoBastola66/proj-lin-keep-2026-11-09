### 1.1 Radiometric Dating: How We Know Earth's Age
The age of the Earth (**$4.54 \pm 0.05\text{ billion years}$**) was determined by Clair Patterson in 1953 using **Uranium-Lead (U-Pb) Isotopic Dating** on the Canyon Diablo iron meteorite (remnants of the primordial solar nebula unaffected by Earth’s subsequent tectonic recycling):
- $^{238}\text{U} \to \, ^{206}\text{Pb}$ (Half-life: $4.468\text{ billion years}$).
- $^{235}\text{U} \to \, ^{207}\text{Pb}$ (Half-life: $704\text{ million years}$).

### 1.2 The Iron Catastrophe: Genesis of Earth's Core
Shortly after accretion, heat from three sources melted the proto-Earth completely:
1. **Kinetic impact energy** from continuous planetesimal bombardment.
2. **Gravitational compression** heating the interior.
3. **Decay of short-lived radioactive isotopes** ($^{26}\text{Al}$ half-life $717,000\text{ years}$, $^{60}\text{Fe}$).
- **Planetary Differentiation**: When the interior reached the melting point of iron ($\sim 1,538^\circ\text{C}$), dense liquid metallic **Iron ($85\%$) and Nickel ($10\%$)** sank under gravity toward the planetary center. Less dense molten **silicate minerals (Magnesium, Silicon, Oxygen, Aluminum)** floated to the top, forming the mantle and primordial magma ocean.
- This gravitational descent released an additional $10^{30}\text{ Joules}$ of gravitational potential energy as heat.

## 2. Earth’s Internal Concentric Architecture

Earth’s internal structure was mapped by seismologists (Andrija Mohorovičić, Beno Gutenberg, Inge Lehmann) tracking the reflection and refraction of seismic **P-waves (Primary, compressional)** and **S-waves (Secondary, shear waves that cannot travel through liquids)**.

### 2.1 The Geodynamo: Earth's Magnetic Shield
- The liquid outer core (temperature $4,000^\circ\text{C}$ to $5,000^\circ\text{C}$) undergoes vigorous **thermal and compositional convection**.
- Coupled with the **Coriolis Effect** from Earth's 24-hour axial rotation, this molten iron flow forms giant helical convection columns aligned with Earth’s rotational axis.
- **Self-Exciting Geodynamo (Joseph Larmor, Walter Elsasser)**: Moving conductive liquid iron across a magnetic field induces electrical currents, which in turn amplify and sustain the dipolar **Geomagnetic Field**.
- **The Magnetosphere**: Deflects energetic charged protons and electrons of the **Solar Wind**, preventing the Sun from stripping away Earth's atmosphere and oceans (the exact fate that destroyed the atmosphere of Mars when its internal geodynamo froze 4 billion years ago).
