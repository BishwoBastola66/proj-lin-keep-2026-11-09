### 1.1 The Iron Catastrophe (4.5 Billion Years Ago)
During planetary accretion, radioactive decay ($^{26}\text{Al}$, $^{60}\text{Fe}$), gravitational compression, and colossal impacts melted the primordial planet. Dense molten iron and nickel sank rapidly toward the gravitational center (**The Iron Catastrophe**), releasing additional gravitational potential energy ($\sim 10^{30}\text{ Joules}$) that drove planetary thermal differentiation into an iron-nickel metallic core and a silicate mantle.

### 1.2 The Geodynamo: Magnetohydrodynamic Engine
Earth's geomagnetic dipole field ($B \approx 25\text{--}65\text{ }\mu\text{T}$) is generated by the **Outer Core** via self-exciting dynamo action:
1. **Convection Drivers**:
   - **Thermal Convection**: Heat flux across the Core-Mantle Boundary (CMB) ($5\text{--}15\text{ TW}$).
   - **Compositional Convection**: As the inner core crystallizes solid iron at $1\text{ mm/year}$, light alloying elements (sulfur, oxygen, silicon, carbon, hydrogen) are excluded into the liquid outer core, rising buoyantly.
2. **Coriolis Helicity & Induction**:
   Planetary rotation organizes convective plumes into helical, spiraling columns aligned with the rotational axis. The induction equation governs magnetic field generation:
   $$\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} = \nabla \times (\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{B}) + \eta \nabla^2 \mathbf{B}$$
   Where $\mathbf{u}$ is fluid velocity and $\eta = \frac{1}{\mu_0 \sigma}$ is magnetic diffusivity. Earth's dynamo protects the biosphere and atmosphere from solar wind sputtering and cosmic rays.

### 2.1 Mercury's Giant Core Anomaly
Mercury has an abnormally high bulk density ($5{,}427\text{ kg/m}^3$). Its metallic core occupies **$85\%$ of the planetary radius** and $\sim 70\%$ of its total mass. Two primary models explain this:
1. **Collisional Stripping**: A proto-planetary giant impact early in the Solar System stripped away most of Mercury's silicate crust and mantle.
2. **Thermal Evaporation**: Early intense solar irradiation vaporized silicate rocks, leaving an iron-dominated planet.
- NASA MESSENGER proved Mercury has a molten outer core layer containing light sulfur ($\sim 5\text{--}10\%$), sustaining a weak active global dynamo ($1\%$ of Earth's field strength).

### 2.2 Mars & The NASA InSight Breakthrough
In 2021–2023, NASA's InSight seismometer recorded marsquakes that mapped the Martian deep interior:
- Mars' core radius is **$1{,}830 \pm 40\text{ km}$** (larger than previously estimated).
- Its low density ($\sim 6{,}000\text{ kg/m}^3$) indicates a high fraction of light elements: **$15\text{--}17\text{ wt}\%$ sulfur**, along with smaller fractions of oxygen, carbon, and hydrogen.
- The Martian dynamo ceased $\sim 3.9\text{--}4.1\text{ billion years ago}$ as the core cooled and stratified, allowing solar wind to strip away the Martian atmosphere and surface water.

### 3.1 Jupiter's Dilute "Fuzzy" Core (NASA Juno Findings)
Data from NASA's Juno spacecraft (2016–present) measuring high-order gravitational harmonics ($J_2, J_4, J_6, J_8$) overturned the classical model of a compact rocky core in Jupiter:
- Jupiter possesses a **dilute (diffuse) core** extending across **$30\text{--}50\%$ of the planet's radius**.
- Silicate minerals and heavy elements do not exist as a sharp, solid central sphere; they are **partially dissolved into supercritical liquid metallic hydrogen**.
- Likely created by a **head-on giant impact with a 10-Earth-mass planetary embryo** during the dawn of the Solar System, shattering the primordial dense core and mixing it with the metallic hydrogen envelope.

### 3.2 Superionic Ice in Uranus and Neptune
Under the extreme interior pressures ($P > 200\text{ GPa}$) and temperatures ($T > 2{,}000\text{--}5{,}000\text{ K}$) of Uranus and Neptune:
- Water exists as **superionic ice ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$)**: Oxygen atoms freeze into a rigid crystalline lattice while hydrogen protons ($\text{H}^+$) diffuse rapidly through the lattice like a liquid.
- This creates an electrical conductor with extraordinary protonic conductivity. Convection within this outer ionic shell generates the wildly tilted, off-center magnetic fields of Uranus ($59^\circ$ tilt) and Neptune ($47^\circ$ tilt).

### 3.3 Dwarf Planets and Ocean Moons
- **Ganymede (Jupiter's Moon)**: The only moon in the Solar System with an **internally generated dipole magnetic field**, powered by a molten liquid iron-sulfur core ($r \sim 500\text{ km}$).
- **Europa & Enceladus**: Differentiated iron-rich/silicate cores surrounded by a rocky mantle, capped by a **$100\text{-km}$ deep liquid saline water ocean** warmed by tidal dissipation, beneath an outer ice crust.
- **Pluto (New Horizons 2015)**: Differentiated world with a dense silicate rocky core ($r \sim 850\text{ km}$, $\sim 70\%$ of its mass), potentially insulated by a liquid water-ammonia subsurface ocean beneath a nitrogen-methane ice shell.

### 4.1 Chthonian Worlds: The Exposed Giant Cores
When gas giants migrate within $0.05\text{ AU}$ of hot stars, hydrodynamic atmospheric escape driven by extreme ultraviolet (XUV) stellar radiation strips the primordial hydrogen-helium envelope completely:
- **TOI-849b**: Discovered with a radius of $3.4\text{ }R_{\oplus}$ but a mass of **$40\text{ }M_{\oplus}$**, yielding a density matching pure rock and iron. It is the **naked, exposed core of a failed gas giant**, offering a direct astronomical observation of an interior core.

### 4.2 Carbon and Diamond Planet Interiors
In protoplanetary disks with high carbon-to-oxygen ratios ($\text{C}/\text{O} > 0.8$), silicon condenses as silicon carbide ($\text{SiC}$) and carbon as graphite/diamond rather than silicate minerals:
- Interiors consist of an iron-silicide/iron-carbide core, a thick **diamond/silicon carbide mantle**, and an outer graphite/hydrocarbon crust.
- Extreme thermal conductivity of crystalline diamond drastically alters planetary convective cooling and plate tectonics.

### 4.3 High-Pressure Electrides in Super-Earth Cores
In Super-Earth cores ($M > 5\text{--}10\text{ }M_{\oplus}$), core-mantle pressures exceed **$1{,}000\text{--}3{,}000\text{ GPa}$ ($10\text{--}30\text{ Mbar}$)**:
- At these ultra-pressures, atomic orbitals deform. Valence electrons are forced out of atomic orbitals into interstitial voids between atomic nuclei, forming **electrides**.
- Silicate minerals become metallic conductors, allowing the planet's deep mantle to generate its own planetary magnetic field independently of the metallic core.
