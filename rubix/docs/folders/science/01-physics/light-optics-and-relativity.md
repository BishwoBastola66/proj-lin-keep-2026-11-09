### 1.1 Classical Wave Theory: James Clerk Maxwell's Unification (1865)
In 1865, Scottish physicist James Clerk Maxwell unified electricity and magnetism into four fundamental differential equations:
1. **Gauss's Law for Electricity**: $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$ (Electric charges produce diverging electric fields).
2. **Gauss's Law for Magnetism**: $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$ (There are no magnetic monopoles).
3. **Faraday's Law of Induction**: $\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$ (Changing magnetic fields induce circulating electric fields).
4. **Ampère-Maxwell Law**: $\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}$ (Electric currents and changing electric fields induce magnetic fields).

In a vacuum with no charges ($\rho = 0$) and no currents ($\mathbf{J} = 0$), combining the curl of Faraday's and Ampère's laws produces the **Electromagnetic Wave Equation**:
$$\nabla^2 \mathbf{E} = \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial^2 \mathbf{E}}{\partial t^2}$$
Maxwell discovered that the speed of this wave $v$ is determined solely by two fundamental physical constants of free space:
$$c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\varepsilon_0 \mu_0}}$$
Where:
- $\varepsilon_0 \approx 8.854 \times 10^{-12}\text{ F/m}$ (Vacuum permittivity).
- $\mu_0 \approx 1.256 \times 10^{-6}\text{ N/A}^2$ (Vacuum permeability).

Evaluating this yielded **$299,792,458\text{ meters per second}$**—identically matching the measured speed of light. Maxwell famously wrote:
> *"We can scarcely avoid the conclusion that light consists in the transverse undulations of the same medium which is the cause of electric and magnetic phenomena."*

### 1.2 The Quantum Reality: The Photon (Einstein, 1905)
Light is not merely a continuous wave. In 1905, Albert Einstein solved the **Photoelectric Effect** (for which he won the 1921 Nobel Prize) by proving that light is emitted, absorbed, and propagated in discrete quantized packets of energy called **photons**:
$$E = h \nu = \hbar \omega$$
- Photons have **zero rest mass** ($m_0 = 0$).
- Photons carry momentum despite having no mass: $p = \frac{E}{c} = \frac{h}{\lambda}$.
- Photons have intrinsic spin angular momentum of $1\hbar$ (vector gauge bosons mediating the electromagnetic force).

## 2. The Speed of Light ($c$) and the Laws of Spacetime

### 2.1 The Invariance of the Speed of Light
In 1887, the **Michelson-Morley Experiment** attempted to measure the velocity of Earth through the hypothetical "luminiferous aether". The result was a definitive null: **light travels at the exact same velocity in all directions regardless of the motion of the Earth or the observer**.

In 1905, Einstein formalized this as the foundational postulate of **Special Relativity**:
> *The speed of light in a vacuum ($c = 299,792,458\text{ m/s}$) is an absolute physical constant, identical in all inertial reference frames, completely independent of the velocity of the emitting source or the receiving observer.*

### 2.2 Time Dilation and Space Contraction (The Laws of Time and Space)
Because $c$ is constant for all observers, **space and time must warp** to preserve this constancy:
1. **Time Dilation**: A clock moving at velocity $v$ relative to an observer ticks slower than a stationary clock:
   $$\Delta t' = \frac{\Delta t}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}} = \gamma \Delta t$$
   As $v \to c$, $\gamma \to \infty$: for an observer traveling at light speed, **time completely stops**. A photon emitted in the early universe 13 billion years ago experiences zero elapsed proper time between its emission and its absorption in a telescope.
2. **Length Contraction**: An object moving at velocity $v$ contracts in the direction of motion:
   $$L' = L \sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}} = \frac{L}{\gamma}$$
3. **Relativistic Mass-Energy Equivalence**:
   $$E = \gamma m_0 c^2 = \frac{m_0 c^2}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}}$$
   For any particle with non-zero rest mass ($m_0 > 0$), as $v \to c$, the energy required to accelerate it approaches **infinity ($\infty$)**. Therefore, **no object with mass can ever reach or exceed the speed of light in a vacuum**.

## 4. General Relativity: Gravity as Spacetime Curvature

In 1915, Albert Einstein published the **General Theory of Relativity**, overturning Isaac Newton's 230-year-old concept of gravity as an invisible attractive force between masses.

### 4.1 The Einstein Field Equations
$$G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}$$
Where:
- $G_{\mu\nu} = R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2} R g_{\mu\nu}$ is the **Einstein Curvature Tensor** describing the geometry of spacetime.
- $g_{\mu\nu}$ is the **Metric Tensor** defining distances in 4D spacetime.
- $\Lambda$ is the **Cosmological Constant** (Dark Energy).
- $T_{\mu\nu}$ is the **Stress-Energy-Momentum Tensor** describing the density, flux, and pressure of matter and energy.
- $G \approx 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ m}^3\text{kg}^{-1}\text{s}^{-2}$ (Newton's gravitational constant).

Physicist John Archibald Wheeler summarized the equations in a single sentence:
> *"Spacetime tells matter how to move; matter tells spacetime how to curve."*

### 4.2 Gravitational Time Dilation
Gravity is not a force pulling on objects; objects simply follow the straightest possible paths (**geodesics**) through curved spacetime. Because gravity curves spacetime:
- **Clocks tick slower near strong gravitational fields**:
  $$t_f = t_0 \sqrt{1 - \frac{2GM}{r c^2}} = t_0 \sqrt{1 - \frac{r_s}{r}}$$
  A clock on Earth's surface ticks slightly slower than a clock in deep space.
- **GPS Satellite Engineering Proof**: GPS satellites orbit 20,200 km above Earth. Due to Special Relativity (satellite velocity $v \approx 3.9\text{ km/s}$), satellite clocks run slower by $7\ \mu\text{s}$ per day. Due to General Relativity (weaker gravity at high altitude), satellite clocks run faster by $45\ \mu\text{s}$ per day. The net effect is that GPS clocks run **$38\ \mu\text{s}$ faster per day**. If engineers did not mathematically correct for Einstein's relativistic time laws, global GPS navigation would accumulate **11 kilometers of error every single day**.
