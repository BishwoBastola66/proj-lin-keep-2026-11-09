PART I: ALL TYPES OF HARDWARE EXISTING IN THE WORLD
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COMPUTE & PROCESSING HARDWARE (SILICON & SUBSTRATES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Central Processing Units (CPUs):
   - x86-64 / AMD64 (Intel Core/Xeon, AMD Ryzen/EPYC)
   - ARM / ARM64 (Apple Silicon M-series/A-series, AWS Graviton, Qualcomm Snapdragon, Cortex)
   - RISC-V (Open-standard instruction set silicon: SiFive, T-Head, ESP32-C3)
   - Legacy & Specialist Mainframe Architectures (IBM z/Architecture, POWER/PowerPC, SPARC, MIPS, Alpha)
2. Graphics Processing Units (GPUs):
   - Datacenter Compute GPUs (Nvidia H100/H200/B200, AMD Instinct MI300X, Intel Gaudi)
   - Consumer Discrete GPUs (Nvidia GeForce RTX series, AMD Radeon RX series, Intel Arc)
   - Integrated GPUs (Intel Xe, AMD RDNA iGPU, Apple unified GPU cores)
3. Tensor, Neural & AI Accelerators (TPUs / NPUs / VPUs):
   - Cloud TPUs (Google TPU v4/v5p/v6e, AWS Trainium/Inferentia)
   - Edge & Client NPUs (Apple Neural Engine, Qualcomm Hexagon, Intel NPU, AMD XDNA)
   - Vision Processing Units (Intel Movidius)
4. Field-Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGAs) & CPLDs:
   - High-End FPGAs (AMD/Xilinx Versal/Virtex UltraScale+, Intel Agilex/Stratix)
   - Embedded & Low-Power FPGAs (Lattice Semiconductor iCE40, Microchip PolarFire)
5. Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (ASICs):
   - Cryptocurrency Mining ASICs (SHA-256, Scrypt dedicated miners)
   - Network Switch ASICs (Broadcom Tomahawk/Jericho, Cisco Silicon One)
   - Video Encoding/Transcoding ASICs (Google VCU, Netint, Quadra)
6. Digital Signal Processors (DSPs) & Image Signal Processors (ISPs):
   - Audio & RF DSPs (Texas Instruments TMS320, Analog Devices SHARC)
   - Camera ISPs (Integrated into smartphone/automotive vision SoCs)
7. Microcontrollers (MCUs) & Microcomputers:
   - 8-bit & 16-bit Microcontrollers (Microchip PIC, Atmel AVR / ATmega328, TI MSP430)
   - 32-bit Cortex-M Microcontrollers (STMicroelectronics STM32, NXP LPC, Nordic nRF52/53)
   - Dual-Core & Wireless MCUs (Espressif ESP32/ESP8266, Raspberry Pi RP2040/RP2350)
8. Quantum Processing Units (QPUs):
   - Superconducting Qubits (IBM Quantum Heron/Eagle, Google Sycamore, Rigetti)
   - Trapped-Ion Processors (IonQ, Quantinuum)
   - Photonic Quantum Processors (Xanadu Borealis, PsiQuantum)
   - Neutral Atom Processors (QuEra, Pasqal)
9. Neuromorphic, Analog & Optical Computing:
   - Spiking Neuromorphic Silicon (Intel Loihi 2, IBM TrueNorth, BrainScaleS, SpiNNaker)
   - Analog In-Memory Computing (Memristor arrays, Mythic AI analog accelerators)
   - Photonic & Optical Matrix Multipliers (Lightmatter Envise, Celestial AI)
10. Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) & Secure Enclaves:
    - Discrete Security Hardware (YubiKey, smartcards, SIM cards, TPM 2.0 chips)
    - Silicon Enclaves (Apple Secure Enclave, ARM TrustZone, AMD PSP, Intel SGX)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MEMORY & DATA STORAGE HARDWARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. Volatile Primary Memory:
    - Static RAM (SRAM): High-speed on-die CPU L1/L2/L3 cache blocks
    - Dynamic RAM (DRAM): System RAM (DDR4, DDR5, LPDDR5X)
    - High Bandwidth Memory (HBM): Stacked 3D TSV DRAM (HBM2e, HBM3, HBM3e)
12. High-Speed Solid-State Non-Volatile Storage:
    - NVMe SSDs (PCIe Gen4 / Gen5 M.2 and U.2/E1.S enterprise flash)
    - Embedded Flash (UFS 4.0 in smartphones, eMMC in embedded devices)
    - Persistent Phase-Change Memory (Intel Optane / 3D XPoint, CXL-attached memory)
13. Magnetic & Mechanical Mass Storage:
    - Hard Disk Drives (HDDs): CMR, SMR, HAMR high-capacity rotating magnetic drives
    - Magnetic Tape Storage: Linear Tape-Open (LTO-9, IBM 3592) archival systems
14. Read-Only & Programmable Non-Volatile Firmware Memory:
    - EEPROM, NOR Flash (SPI boot flash), NAND Flash (SLC, MLC, TLC, QLC, PLC)
15. Emerging & Long-Term Archival Media:
    - 5D Optical Glass Storage (Project Silica)
    - Synthetic DNA Molecular Storage
    - MRAM (Magnetoresistive RAM) and FeRAM (Ferroelectric RAM)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. INTERCONNECTS, BUSES & NETWORKING HARDWARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. System Busses & Inter-Chip Fabrics:
    - Peripheral Component Interconnect Express (PCIe Gen 3, 4, 5, 6)
    - Compute Express Link (CXL 1.1, 2.0, 3.0 memory pooling fabrics)
    - High-Speed Accelerator Interconnects (Nvidia NVLink / NVSwitch, AMD Infinity Fabric)
17. Datacenter & Enterprise Network Hardware:
    - InfiniBand Adapters & Switches (HDR, NDR 400G, XDR 800G)
    - High-Speed Ethernet NICs & SmartNICs / DPUs (Nvidia BlueField, Intel Mount Evans)
    - Optical Transceivers (QSFP-DD, OSFP, Co-Packaged Optics - CPO)
18. Telecommunications & Cellular Infrastructure:
    - 5G / 6G Baseband Units, Remote Radio Heads (RRH), Massive MIMO arrays
    - Satellite Ground Terminals & Phased Array Antennas (Starlink Dishy, Kuiper)
19. Wireless Local & Personal Area Network Hardware:
    - Wi-Fi Chipsets (Wi-Fi 6, 6E, Wi-Fi 7 802.11be)
    - Bluetooth & Low-Energy transceivers (Bluetooth 5.4, LE Audio)
    - Ultra-Wideband (UWB), Zigbee, Z-Wave, Thread, LoRaWAN long-range radios
20. Embedded, Automotive & Industrial Interfaces:
    - Controller Area Network (CAN bus, CAN-FD), FlexRay, LIN in automotive
    - Serial Peripheral Interface (SPI), I2C, UART, RS-485, Modbus, Profibus

4. SENSORS, ACTUATORS, PHYSICAL PERIPHERALS & POWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
21. Optical, Spatial & Environmental Sensors:
    - LiDAR (Solid-state, mechanical rotating), RADAR (Millimeter-wave automotive radar)
    - CMOS Image Sensors (Sony Exmor, Samsung ISOCELL), Thermal / Infrared cameras
    - Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs: 3-axis/6-axis/9-axis gyroscopes & accelerometers)
    - Time-of-Flight (ToF) depth sensors, Ultrasonic rangefinders, Barometers
22. Biomedical & Neural Interfaces:
    - Electrocardiogram (ECG), Photoplethysmography (PPG heart rate), Pulse Oximetry
    - Neural recording electrodes (Utah array, Neuralink N1 implant, EEG caps)
23. Actuators, Motion & Robotics Hardware:
    - Brushless DC (BLDC) motors, Stepper motors, Servomotors, Linear actuators
    - Hydraulic and pneumatic industrial robotics manifolds
24. Displays & Human-Machine Interfaces:
    - MicroLED, OLED, AMOLED, Mini-LED, Liquid Crystal (IPS/VA LCD)
    - Electronic Paper Displays (E-Ink Carta / Gallery)
    - Spatial AR/VR Micro-OLED displays & Waveguide optical combiners
    - Haptic feedback actuators (Linear Resonant Actuators - LRA, Piezo haptics)
25. Thermal & Power Delivery Silicon:
    - Power Management ICs (PMICs), Voltage Regulator Modules (VRMs)
    - Gallium Nitride (GaN) and Silicon Carbide (SiC) power semiconductors
    - Liquid cooling distribution units (CDUs), Immersion cooling tanks, Vapor chambers
26. Extreme Environment & Mission-Critical Hardware:
    - Radiation-Hardened Aerospace Silicon (BAE RAD750, Vorago ARM, SpaceWire)
    - Deep-bore geological telemetry chips (rated for 200°C+ and high shock)
